Gym DOOH Penetration
SA gyms — largely untapped
Digital Out-of-Home advertising is one of the fastest-growing media channels in South Africa. Yet gyms — venues with captive, affluent, health-conscious audiences — remain almost entirely unmonetised from an advertising perspective.
Gym members are a premium demographic. LSM 7–10. High disposable income. Brands pay a premium to reach them — but currently have no scalable, measurable way to do it inside gym environments.
The Gap

Gyms have screens. Advertisers want audiences. Nobody has built the network to connect them — until now.

What We Built

GymGaze installs digital screens across gym venues and manages them as a unified advertising network — from content scheduling to campaign reporting — through proprietary software.
1
Install Screens

Automated Proof of Flight reports. Agencies pay in 45 days. GymGaze shares revenue with venue.
The Competitive Moat

GymGaze owns its own technology stack — the GymGaze platform + Cuecast CMS. Most DOOH operators rent third-party software. We built ours. That means lower operating costs, full control, and a proprietary asset that grows in value with every venue added.
